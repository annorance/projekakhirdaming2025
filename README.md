This study applies machine learning methods—such as Logistic Regression, SVM, and XGBoost—to classify depression in students. The model was also chosen for having the highest recall score at 90%, indicating its optimal capability in identifying individuals at risk of mental health disorders. In the context of medical detection, a high recall rate plays a crucial role in minimizing false negatives—instances where individuals who actually have the condition (positive cases) are incorrectly classified as healthy (negative cases). Such misclassifications are highly risky as they may lead to delayed treatment. Therefore, improving recall directly supports early detection effectiveness and ensures more targeted medical interventions.

Considering the overall evaluation results, SVM is regarded as offering the most optimal trade-off between accuracy, performance stability, and generalization capability, making it the most suitable model for real-world implementation.
